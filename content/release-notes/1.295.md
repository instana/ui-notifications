## Features

The following features are added in release 1.0.295:

### Support for W3C headers in mobile agents

Mobile agents, such as [android-agent (6.2.1 and later)](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/instana-observability/1.0.295?topic=applications-android-api#setting-up-instana-monitoring), [iOS-agent (1.9.1 and later)](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/instana-observability/1.0.295?topic=applications-ios-api#setting-up-the-instana-ios-agent), [react-native-agent(2.0.8 and later)](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/instana-observability/1.0.295?topic=applications-react-native-api#backend-correlation-with-w3cheaders), and [flutter-agent (3.1.1 and later)](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/instana-observability/1.0.295?topic=applications-flutter-monitoring-api#backend-correlation-with-w3cheaders), now support W3C-compliant headers in requests to the backend. Instana can now monitor mobile apps with OpenTelemetry-instrumented backends, even without the Instana agent on the backend. This enhancement broadens the range of applications that you can monitor, ensuring complete system visibility regardless of the backend's instrumentation.

## Improvements

The following improvements are provided in release 1.0.295:

### Cookie consent management

The cookie consent management feature in Instana is now moved from the **Settings** menu to the **Profile** menu. To view and update your cookie preferences, go to the **Profile** menu and click **Privacy**. TrustArc is used for cookie consent management and it helps to ensure compliance with global data privacy regulations by enabling users to make informed choices about their data. For more information, see [Privacy and compliance](https://w3.ibm.com/w3publisher/digital-behavior-data-management/privacy-and-compliance) and [Overview on Tealium and TrustArc](https://w3.ibm.com/w3publisher/digital-behavior-data-management/tag-management/tealium-overview).
